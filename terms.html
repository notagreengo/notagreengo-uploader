NotaGreengo Uploader — Terms of Service

Last updated: October 21, 2026

NotaGreengo Uploader is a private desktop utility used by its owner to prepare and upload the owner's own short-form video content to supported social platforms.

Use of the app

The app is intended only for accounts explicitly authorized by the account owner. The user is responsible for ensuring that uploaded content complies with the rules and policies of each connected platform.

Content and credentials

Media files remain on the user's device until the user explicitly initiates an upload. Platform credentials and authorization tokens are used only to connect the user's authorized accounts and perform requested publishing actions.

No resale or public service

This app is not offered as a public social-media management service and is not intended to provide access to third parties.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the app changes.
