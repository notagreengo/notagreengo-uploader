NotaGreengo Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

NotaGreengo Uploader is a private desktop utility used by its owner to upload the owner's own content to authorized social-media accounts.

Data the app uses

The app may use platform account identifiers, access tokens, refresh tokens, publishing metadata, captions, and local media-file paths as required to authenticate and publish content.

Local storage

Media files are kept on the user's local device until the user explicitly starts an upload. The app does not operate a public media-hosting service.

Third-party platforms

When the user chooses to publish content, the app sends the selected media and publishing metadata directly to the authorized platform using that platform's API. The platform then handles that data under its own privacy policy and terms.

Sharing and sale of data

The app does not sell personal data and is not designed to share account data with unrelated third parties.

Revoking access

The user can revoke platform authorization through the connected platform's account or developer settings.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as the app changes.
